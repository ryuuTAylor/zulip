In addition to [scheduling a single message](/help/schedule-a-message),
you can schedule a message that **repeats** on a daily, weekly, or
monthly pattern, or **send the same message to multiple destinations**
in one scheduled job. Both options share a single **Schedule message**
dialog and can be combined — for example, a weekly status update sent
to two channels and one direct-message group at the same time.

<ZulipNote>
  This feature is available to every Zulip member. There are no
  administrator-only controls or per-organization settings. Each user
  can only view, edit, and cancel their own scheduled messages.
</ZulipNote>

## Open the scheduler

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <StartComposing />

      1. Click the **ellipsis** (<MoreVerticalIcon />) next to the
         **Send** (<SendIcon />) button.
      1. Click **Schedule message**.
    </FlattenedSteps>

    The **Schedule message** dialog opens with fields for the message
    content, send time, optional recurrence, and one or more
    destinations.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Schedule a recurring message

A recurring message is delivered repeatedly on a schedule you choose,
until you cancel it.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      1. Open the **Schedule message** dialog (see [above](#open-the-scheduler)).
      1. Write your message in the **Message** field.
      1. Check **Repeat** to reveal the recurrence options.
      1. From the **Repeats** dropdown, choose **Daily**, **Weekly**,
         or **Monthly**.
      1. Set the delivery **Time**.
      1. Configure the pattern as described below.
      1. Add at least one destination (see
         [Schedule to multiple destinations](#schedule-to-multiple-destinations)).
      1. Click **Schedule**.
    </FlattenedSteps>

    The **Send at** field is ignored when **Repeat** is checked — the
    next delivery is computed from the recurrence pattern instead.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

### Weekly pattern

Under **Weekly on**, check the boxes for each weekday the message
should repeat on. You must pick at least one weekday. The message will
be delivered on each selected weekday at the configured time.

### Monthly pattern

Under **Monthly on**, choose one of three patterns:

- **On the `<day>` day of the month** — delivers on the calendar day
  you pick. For months without that day (e.g., **31** in February),
  Zulip falls back to the last available day of that month.
- **On the last day of the month** — delivers on the last calendar
  day, automatically adjusting for month length and leap years.
- **On the `<ordinal>` `<weekday>`** — delivers on the _n_-th
  occurrence of a weekday (e.g., "first Monday", "last Friday"). If a
  month has
  fewer than four occurrences of the chosen weekday, that month is
  skipped.

<ZulipTip>
  A live summary of the monthly pattern appears below the options as
  you change them, so you can confirm the schedule before submitting.
</ZulipTip>

## Schedule to multiple destinations

A scheduled message — one-time or recurring — can be delivered to any
combination of channels and direct-message groups in a single job.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      1. Open the **Schedule message** dialog.
      1. Write your message.
      1. *(optional)* Check **Repeat** and configure recurrence.
      1. *(optional)* If not repeating, set **Send at** to the
         one-time delivery time.
      1. Add destinations one at a time:
         - **For a channel:** pick a channel from **— Select a channel —**,
           type a topic, and click **+ Add channel**.
         - **For a direct message:** type a user's name in the
           direct-message field, select them from the suggestions
           (repeat for additional recipients), and click
           **+ Add direct message**.
      1. Each added destination appears as a chip below
         **Destinations**. Remove a destination with the **×** button
         on its chip.
      1. Click **Schedule**.
    </FlattenedSteps>

    Zulip rejects duplicate destinations — adding the same channel and
    topic twice, or the same direct-message recipient set twice, will
    show an inline error.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Combine recurring and batch

To deliver the same recurring message to multiple destinations, check
**Repeat**, configure the recurrence, and add multiple destinations
before clicking **Schedule**. The recurrence runs once per occurrence,
and on each occurrence the same message is delivered to every
destination in the list.

## View, edit, or cancel a scheduled message

Recurring and batch scheduled messages appear in the same
**Scheduled messages** view as one-time scheduled messages.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToScheduledMessages />
    </FlattenedSteps>

    Recurring messages show their schedule pattern (e.g. "Every Monday
    at 9:00 AM") and their next scheduled delivery time. Batch messages
    show every destination they will be delivered to.

    To cancel a recurring message, click the **delete** icon on its
    row. Canceling a recurring message stops all future deliveries; it
    does not unsend any messages that have already been delivered.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  Editing a recurring or batch message is not supported directly —
  cancel the existing schedule and create a new one with the updated
  content or destinations.
</ZulipTip>
